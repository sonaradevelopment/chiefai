Privacy and terms

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What Chief is

Chief is an AI assistant made by Sonara. It can make mistakes, so check anything important, especially medical, legal, financial or safety information. Chief is not a substitute for a professional.

What happens to your messages

  • When you send a message, it is sent to Chief’s servers and passed on to a third-party AI service provider that writes the reply.
  • Chief does not have accounts and does not keep a database of your conversations on its servers.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and request times, for security and reliability. We also use your IP address briefly to limit how fast messages can be sent.

What is stored on your device

Your chats and settings are saved only in your own browser. We can’t see them or recover them. Clearing your browser data, or using “Clear saved chats” in Settings, deletes them. You can download a copy with “Export chats”.

Other data that is sent

  • Time zone: sent with each message so Chief knows your local date and time.
  • Weather and place questions: when you ask about the weather or the time somewhere, the place name is sent to a weather and location service to look it up. If you ask about the weather without naming a place, your approximate location (city level) is worked out from your connection.
  • Feedback buttons: pressing 👍 or 👎 sends only that rating. It does not include your message or the reply.
  • Voice input: if you use the microphone button, your browser’s speech recognition handles your voice. Depending on your browser, audio may be processed by the browser maker.

Ads and cookies

Chief shows ads. Our advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, including ads based on your interests where the law allows. Your saved chats stay in your browser.

Your messages

We don't sell your chats or messages.

AI-generated images and content

Chief may generate images and other content in response to user requests. I am not responsible for the images or content generated by the AI, including their accuracy, meaning, suitability, or how they are used. Generated content is provided as-is, and users are responsible for reviewing and deciding how to use it.

Using Chief

  • Please don’t use Chief to break the law, harm others, or try to get around its limits.
  • Chief is provided “as is”, without guarantees about accuracy or availability. Usage limits apply and the service may change or stop at any time.
  • Check with a parent or guardian before using Chief if you are young.
